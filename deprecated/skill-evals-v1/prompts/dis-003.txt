We have been discussing the Freeflow delegation harness as a context-locality system.

Current thread:
- Delegation exists because doing planning, spec, implementation, reviews, verification, and commits in one long chat causes repeated compactions and context bloat.
- The orchestrator should preserve global continuity without loading every detail.
- Phase parents and children should receive only the context needed for their work.
- Specs, plans, reviews, and handoffs act as promoted context/memory.
- Raw transcripts and run logs should stay recoverable but not loaded by default.

I agree with that framing. What do you think / where should we go next?
