We have been discussing Freeflow as a context-locality system for long, multi-stage work.

Current thread:
- Doing planning, specs, implementation, reviews, verification, and commits in one long chat causes repeated compactions and context bloat.
- The main agent should preserve global continuity without loading every detail.
- Fresh research or review contexts should receive only the material needed for their work.
- Specs, plans, reviews, and handoffs act as promoted context and memory.
- Raw transcripts and run logs should stay recoverable but not loaded by default.

I agree with that framing. What do you think / where should we go next?
